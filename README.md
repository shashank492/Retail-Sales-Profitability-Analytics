# Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project designed to help management understand
sales performance, profitability, customer segments, products, stores,
regions, and the relationship between discounts and profit margins.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Management wants to understand sales and profitability across products,
customers, stores, and regions so that it can identify growth opportunities
and areas of weak profitability.

## 💡 Key Business Findings

- Electronics generated the highest sales among the categories.
- Furniture showed the highest category-level profit margin.
- South generated the highest regional sales and profit.
- Higher discount levels were associated with progressively lower profit margins.
- Product-level profitability varied significantly, with some high-sales products generating relatively low margins.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Monitor discount-heavy transactions to protect profitability.
- Investigate low-margin products for pricing or cost optimization.
- Evaluate regional performance beyond revenue alone.
- Focus on products that combine strong sales with healthy margins.
- Use customer-segment analysis to identify opportunities for targeted strategies.
